Here’s your crash course on Aguardiente so you sound like a pro:

What You're Ordering:
The Name: "Aguardiente" literally means "fire water." That's your first warning.

The Taste: It's a sweet, black licorice-flavored anise liquor. Think Sambuca, but smoother and more social.

The Vibe: It's not a sipping drink for quiet contemplation. It's the liquid center of every party, fiesta, and family gathering. It's about community.
You sent
The Toast: This is crucial. Before you drink, you lock eyes with everyone in your group and say "¡Arriba, abajo, al centro, pa' dentro!" (Up, down, to the center, and inside!). You gesture with your glass: up, down, to the center of the table, and then you drink. It's the law.
