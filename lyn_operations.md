### **Your 3-Point Reset Plan**

#### **1. Reconfigure Your Workspace (Geometry First)**
The rule is simple: **Your client’s hand should be at or slightly below your elbow when you sit upright.** If you have to hike your shoulders to reach, their hand is too high.
- **If your table is fixed:** Lower the client. Use a sturdy stool or even a thick book under their arm rest.
- **Bridge the gap:** Place a folded towel under your client’s wrist. This brings their nails closer to you, so you’re not stretching forward all day.
- **Support your spine:** Sit all the way back in your chair. If needed, roll up a towel and place it behind your lower back. Your core is there to help—let it.

#### **2. Move with Intention (The Micro-Habits)**
Your body remembers patterns. We’re just updating them.
- **Between clients:** Stand up. Do 5 slow shoulder rolls backward. Let your arms dangle and shake out your hands.
- **While working:** Every 20 minutes, pause for 20 seconds. Look across the room, let your shoulders drop, and take three deep breaths. This resets your neck and eyes.
- **Engage your back:** Before you start a nail, gently pull your shoulder blades down and together—like you’re tucking them into your back pockets. This wakes up the muscles that have been napping.

#### **3. The 5-Minute Daily Recharge (Non-Negotiable)**
This is where you undo the day’s tension and build resilience.
- **Wall Angels (to reset your posture):** Stand against a wall. Make a “goalpost” with your arms, backs of hands against the wall. Slowly slide arms up and down, keeping contact. 10 reps. This teaches your shoulders and spine where they should be.
- **Doorway Stretch (to open your chest):** Place forearms on a doorframe, step through gently. Hold for 30 seconds. This counters the forward hunch.
- **Tennis Ball Release (for instant relief):** Put a tennis ball in a sock. Lean against a wall with the ball between your shoulder blade and spine. Find a tight spot, lean in gently, and breathe for 30 seconds. This melts knots.

---

**Start here:** Tomorrow, just focus on **lowering the client’s hand** and doing the **5-minute recharge** after work. Your muscles are ready to be awakened—you just have to give them the signal.

You’ve got the strength and the smarts. This is just about directing them toward your own comfort. Listen to your body—it’s your most important tool.

Respectfully yours in clean lines and pain-free days. 💅✨
